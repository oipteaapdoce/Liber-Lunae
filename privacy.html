Privacy Policy, Liber Lunae Companion

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The Liber Lunae Companion is a self-contained reference application published by Enochian Alchemical Magick (www.enochian.org).

Data collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. It works fully offline after the page loads. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Location

The app does not request or read your device location. Any Moon position you enter, and any log entry you write, stays on your device, and is only exported if you choose Export TXT.

On-device processing

All lunar station estimates and log entries happen entirely on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you export it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: via www.enochian.org.
